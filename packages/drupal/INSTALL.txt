
phpClaw for Drupal
===================

Requirements: PHP 8.1+, Drupal 10 or 11.

1. composer require phpclaw/phpclaw-drupal
2. drush en phpclaw -y
3. Visit Configuration > phpClaw AI Agent > Settings
   (/admin/config/phpclaw/settings), pick a provider, and add your API key.

Full documentation: https://phpclaw.ai/docs/adapters/drupal

For general Drupal installation instructions, see core/INSTALL.txt.
